**Overall Architecture & Aesthetic**
The website is a single-page scrolling experience built with modern HTML, CSS, and JS, utilizing a "Glassmorphism" aesthetic layered over a deep, obsidian-blue animated background (#0B0E14). The background features a subtle, slow-moving grid of glowing nodes. The central hook of the UI is the "Digital Pet"—an elegant, abstract 3D geometric creature (rendered via WebGL/Three.js or smooth CSS/SVG animations) fixed to the right side of the viewport. As the user scrolls down the page, passing different projects, the pet "eats" the data from the scrolling sections, evolving in real-time. It grows from a simple glowing orb into a complex, multi-faceted digital organism, integrating visual motifs from each programming skill and project it passes. 

**Header Section: The Genesis**
The top of the page features a clean, left-aligned typography block. The primary header displays "Geva Kipper" in a bold, modern sans-serif font (like Inter or Space Grotesk). Below it, a sleek sentence reads: "Software Engineer currently solving complex problems and building the future at Google." Beneath this is the dynamic subtitle, styled with a subtle typewriter or glitch effect, reading: "This website is a living digital organism, re-designed each day by AI and thus ever-changing." 
Beside this text, the Digital Pet starts in its "Level 1" state—a pulsing, minimalist geometric egg. A visual cue prompts the user to "Scroll to evolve."

**The Evolution Track (Projects Layout)**
The 8 required sections are designed as a vertical timeline of floating glass cards down the left side of the screen, leaving the right side dedicated to the evolving pet. Each card features a distinct hover effect that sends a stream of glowing data particles toward the pet.

*   **Box 1: GlglStats** – The card background features a subtle, stylized visualization of LZ77 compression blocks and audio waves. Text presents the Python project measuring the repetitiveness of Israeli pop-chart songs. Two sleek, minimalist buttons are included: one linking to the live site (https://glglstats.geekip.dev) and a YouTube play-button icon linking to the PyData TLV conference presentation (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jrKAtOsj1Lo). *Pet Evolution:* The pet consumes a "Python rune" and sprouts a tail made of compression blocks.
*   **Box 2: Ahuzat Dibuk** – The card utilizes a dark red-to-green heatmap gradient border, presenting the Python scraping and Tel-Aviv parking lot occupancy project. It includes three news-ticker style badge links to Channel 13 (https://13tv.co.il/item/news/domestic/internal/parking-902956407/), Mako (https://www.mako.co.il/nexter-news/Article-ae43964b891bf71027.htm), and the original Facebook publication (https://www.facebook.com/groups/secrettelaviv/posts/10159636301195943/). *Pet Evolution:* The pet gains a rotating radar antenna and glowing data-scraping eyes.
*   **Box 3: 2D Part Orienting** – The card background features slowly shifting, intersecting 2D polygons. Text introduces the C++ computational geometry library implementation for constructing polygon push plans. A geometric link button directs to the code and info (https://www.cgl.cs.tau.ac.il/projects/2d-part-orienting/). *Pet Evolution:* The pet’s body shifts from a soft orb into a sleek, sharp, and highly optimized 2D/3D polygon structure.
*   **Box 4: The Turing Jest** – The card is styled like a neon cyber-arena with split red and blue accents. It presents the LMSYS-like arena where LLMs compete at making and explaining puns, rated by users. A glowing link directs to the arena (https://jest.geekip.dev). *Pet Evolution:* The pet splits into two interacting entities or gains a digital "comedy mask" face that occasionally flashes pun-related emojis.
*   **Box 5: Rhymes of Israel** – The card integrates a visual mix of digital newsprint and high-school yearbook scribbles. It describes the Chrome extension using an LLM to rewrite Israeli news headlines into comedic rhymes. A stylized "Add to Chrome" button links to the extension (https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/eainginjgdkpgolpjniijdihfbeeeklb). *Pet Evolution:* The pet begins emitting tiny, floating speech bubbles filled with rhyming binary code.
*   **Box 6: Hatul Basak** – The card features subtle Wikipedia puzzle-globe pieces and a feline silhouette watermark. It introduces the Hebrew port of catfishing.net, featuring AI-generated daily challenges and user-created themed trivia puzzles. A playful paw-print button links to the game (https://hatulbasak.geekip.dev). *Pet Evolution:* The pet sprouts distinct digital cat ears and whisker-sensors, purring with a subtle visual vibration effect.
*   **Box 7: Meta Portfolio** – The card design utilizes a recursive infinity loop graphic, playfully acknowledging the meta-nature of the portfolio website project. A self-reference button links back to the site itself (https://geekip.dev). *Pet Evolution:* The fully evolved pet now holds a miniature, glowing hologram of itself in its paws/appendages.
*   **Box 8: Digital Habitat (Socials)** – The final box acts as the pet's resting place. It features three large, elegant, glowing icons linking to GitHub (github.com/gevak), LinkedIn (linkedin.com/in/gevakip), and Facebook (facebook.com/geva.kipper). The fully evolved pet gracefully hovers above these icons, occasionally interacting with the user's mouse cursor.
