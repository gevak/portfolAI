**Global Theme and Atmosphere**
The website is a single-page parallax experience set against a deep, twilight-to-midnight gradient background (`#0A0A12` to `#000000`). At the very bottom center of the initial viewport burns a dynamic, continuous CSS/WebGL campfire. The core visual mechanic relies on interactive particle physics: hundreds of glowing, semi-transparent orange and gold sparks constantly rise from the fire. As the user scrolls down the page, the "camera" effectively moves upward, following the smoke and sparks into the night sky. The project "boxes" are hidden in the darkness until the rising sparks collide with their bounding areas, instantly illuminating the cards like embers catching fire to reveal the content.

**Hero Section (The Campfire Base)**
The viewport opens on the crackling fire. Hovering elegantly in the smoke just above the flames is a crisp, modern typography lockup.
*   **Headline:** "Geva Kipper" in a bold, warm-white sans-serif font, softly backlit by an orange glow.
*   **Description:** A sleek, minimalist sentence below the name reading: "Software Engineer at Google, building intelligent, scalable, and beautifully unconventional systems."
*   **Subtitle:** Written in a slightly muted, italicized serif font to evoke a sense of fleeting art: *"This digital landscape is dreamed anew each day by AI—an ever-changing canvas."*

**The Spark-Revealed Project Cards (Sections 1-7)**
As the user scrolls, they follow the trail of sparks. The project boxes are not traditional squares; they are designed as "glassmorphism" cards (heavily blurred backgrounds with translucent dark-grey fills and thin, glowing amber borders). They alternate organically left and right along a central invisible sinusoidal path, resembling the chaotic but beautiful flow of a smoke plume. When a card enters the center of the viewport, surrounding background sparks gravitate toward it, intensifying its border glow and fading in the text. 

*   **Box 1 - GlglStats:** Features a faint background watermark of a soundwave. The text describes the Python/LZ77 project measuring the repetitiveness of Israeli pop-chart songs and visualizing the patterns. Below the text, two glowing pill-shaped buttons are provided: one linking to the live site (`https://glglstats.geekip.dev`) and another accompanied by a play icon linking to the PyData TLV presentation (`https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jrKAtOsj1Lo`).
*   **Box 2 - Ahuzat Dibuk:** Features a subtle thermal heat-map gradient bleeding from the edges. The text details the Python scraping and data visualization project for Tel-Aviv parking lot occupancy. The links are styled as a neat row of "As featured in" badges, linking to Channel 13 (`https://13tv.co.il/...`), Mako (`https://www.mako.co.il/...`), and the viral Secret Tel-Aviv Facebook post (`https://www.facebook.com/...`).
*   **Box 3 - 2D Part Orienting:** The visual motif here is a shifting, rotating wireframe polygon that reacts to mouse movement. The text explains the C++ implementation within an open-source computational geometry library for constructing a 'push plan'. A stark, technical-looking button links to the CGL TAU project page (`https://www.cgl.cs.tau.ac.il/projects/2d-part-orienting/`).
*   **Box 4 - The Turing Jest:** The card is adorned with a minimalist neon outline of comedy and tragedy masks. The text describes the LMSYS-like arena where LLMs compete in making and explaining puns, rated by users. A glowing button urges the user to "Enter the Arena" (`https://jest.geekip.dev`).
*   **Box 5 - Rhymes of Israel:** Styled slightly like a burnt piece of a high school yearbook page. The copy explains the Chrome extension that uses an LLM to rewrite Israeli news headlines into comedic rhymes. A prominent button with a Chrome logo accent links to the Chrome Web Store (`https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/...`).
*   **Box 6 - Hatul Basak:** Features a playful, abstract node-graph silhouette of a cat. The text introduces the Hebrew port of catfishing.net, explaining the AI-generated daily challenges and user-created themed Wikipedia trivia puzzles. A split-button layout offers links to "Play Daily Challenge" and "Create a Puzzle" (`https://hatulbasak.geekip.dev`).
*   **Box 7 - The Meta Portfolio:** The card has a mirrored CSS effect, reflecting the page's own rising sparks. The text playfully breaks the fourth wall, presenting the very portfolio website the user is currently navigating. A self-referential infinity-symbol link points to (`https://geekip.dev`).

**Section 8 (The Zenith / Social Links)**
At the highest point of the scroll, the smoke dissipates, and the sparks transition into a starry night sky. The 8th box abandons the glass card look entirely. Instead, it presents three radiant "stars" (icons for GitHub, LinkedIn, and Facebook). When the user hovers over any icon, glowing constellation lines instantly connect them.
*   GitHub: `github.com/gevak`
*   LinkedIn: `linkedin.com/in/gevakip`
*   Facebook: `facebook.com/geva.kipper`

**The Footer (AI Credit & Chronometer)**
Anchored subtly at the absolute bottom of the document (or functioning as a sticky footer that appears once the user reaches the stars). The design switches to a highly precise, terminal-like monospace font glowing in a soft, fading ember-red. 
*   **Credit:** "Forged in the digital fires by an AI Model."
*   **Stopwatch:** Beside the credit, a live JavaScript counter ticks upwards, displaying the exact Hours:Minutes:Seconds elapsed since `2026-08-01 02:08:49`. The numbers flip with a mechanical, retro-digital animation, reinforcing the theme of an ever-shifting, time-bound digital existence.
