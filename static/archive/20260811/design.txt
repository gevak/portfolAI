**Global Theme & Mechanics**
The website is a single-page parallax scrolling experience that simulates a deep-sea dive. As the user scrolls down, the background transitions through a continuous CSS gradient, starting from a sunlit aquamarine surface, plunging into deep teal, passing through indigo, and finally settling into a pitch-black abyssal trench illuminated only by bioluminescence. Ascending bubbles made of curly braces `{}`, semicolons, and binary code drift upwards in the background. Instead of standard rectangular divs, the eight requested project sections are designed as "glassmorphic" diving bells or translucent underwater research screens—featuring frosted glass backgrounds, blurred backdrops, and glowing borders—ensuring text remains highly legible against the dark ocean gradients. Hovering over any link or container triggers a liquid ripple animation or a burst of bioluminescent glow.

**Hero Section (The Surface & The Drop-off)**
The top of the page represents the water's surface, where rays of sunlight penetrate the waves. 
Typography is large, modern, and sans-serif. Front and center is the developer's name, **Geva Kipper**, suspended above the water line. Just beneath the surface, a crisp, elegant sentence reads: "Software Engineer currently building and innovating at Google." Below this, styled in a subtle, shifting "glitch" or "typing" effect to mimic a live terminal, is the subtitle: "This website is uniquely re-designed every day by AI. Expect the unexpected." A glowing, animated downward arrow shaped like an anchor or a chevron prompts the user to begin their descent.

**Tier 1: The Sunlit Zone (Depth: 10m - Data & Rhythms)**
The water here is bright blue and teeming with life.
*Box 1 (GlglStats):* Designed like a high-tech sonar display surrounded by a colorful coral reef made of vertical bar charts. The description highlights the Python LZ77 pop-chart project. It includes a sleek button linking to `https://glglstats.geekip.dev` and an embedded thumbnail shaped like a submarine window linking to the PyData TLV YouTube video. 
*Box 2 (Ahuzat Dibuk):* Placed slightly lower and offset to the other side. This box incorporates a thermal-camera aesthetic, mimicking the parking lot heatmaps. Small fish swim in dense, tight patterns around it, representing traffic. Links to the Channel 13 and Mako news stories, as well as the Secret Tel Aviv Facebook post, are styled as glowing waypoint markers.

**Tier 3: The Twilight Zone (Depth: 200m - Logic & Puns)**
The background transitions to a moody, dark teal. Sunlight is fading.
*Box 3 (2D Part Orienting):* The container is flanked by floating, glowing geometric jellyfish whose tentacles form intricate, moving polygons. The C++ computational geometry project is described here with a stark, terminal-style monospace font emphasizing efficiency. A glowing tether links to `https://www.cgl.cs.tau.ac.il/projects/2d-part-orienting/`.
*Box 4 (The Turing Jest):* Positioned opposite Box 3, this section features two glowing anglerfish facing off in the dark, their bright lures representing competing LLMs in an LMSYS-like arena. The playful, pun-focused text is accompanied by an interactive-looking button linking to `https://jest.geekip.dev`.

**Tier 4: The Midnight Zone (Depth: 1000m - Words & Trivia)**
The water is deep indigo. Bioluminescence is the only light source.
*Box 5 (Rhymes of Israel):* This box is illuminated by a digital octopus spraying glowing, neon-colored ink that morphs into Hebrew rhyming couplets. The Chrome extension description is paired with a prominent "Install Extension" button linking to the Chrome Web Store.
*Box 6 (Hatul Basak):* A surreal, AI-generated "cat-fish" (feline head, aquatic body) swims slowly behind the frosted glass of this container. The trivia game description glows in a soft, Wikipedia-puzzle-piece white, with a button directing users to `https://hatulbasak.geekip.dev` to play and create daily puzzles.

**Tier 5: The Abyss (Depth: 4000m - The Core)**
The background is pitch black, illuminated strictly by matrix-green and neon-blue glowing elements.
*Box 7 (Portfolio Website):* A self-referential, meta container designed to look like a sunken, high-tech server rack humming with power on the ocean floor. The text breaks the fourth wall, acknowledging the site itself, with a glowing fiber-optic cable linking back to `https://geekip.dev`.
*Box 8 (Social Links):* Designed as a glowing, open treasure chest resting on digital sand. Instead of gold, the chest emanates light, with sleek, modern icons for GitHub, LinkedIn, and Facebook floating upward like shiny pearls, linking to `github.com/gevak`, `linkedin.com/in/gevakip`, and `facebook.com/geva.kipper`.

**Footer (The Mariana Trench)**
At the absolute bottom of the page, the ocean floor is made of a topological wireframe mesh. Centered in a muted, neon-cyan monospace font is a credit line reading: "Depths charted and designed by [AI Model Name]." Below this sits a digital UI element styled like a submarine's pressure gauge or dive timer: a live JavaScript stopwatch actively counting up the exact hours, minutes, and seconds that have elapsed since `2026-08-11 01:09:54`, grounding the ever-changing AI design in a continuous flow of time.
