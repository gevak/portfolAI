**Overall Layout & Atmosphere**
The website operates as a full-screen, interactive digital camera viewfinder. The overarching aesthetic is a sleek, dark-mode Heads-Up Display (HUD) utilizing a deep charcoal background with crisp, glowing white and subtle red UI accents. The screen is framed by subtle cinematic letterboxing. When the user moves their mouse, the entire background canvas shifts slightly on a 3D parallax axis to simulate camera sway, while a faint, blurred "real world" environment sits far in the background, out of focus.

**The Fixed HUD (Heads-Up Display)**
Overlaying the entire viewport is the fixed camera UI. In the center, a thin, minimalist rule-of-thirds grid and a central crosshair `[ + ]` remain static. 
In the top-left corner, replacing standard camera specs (like ISO or aperture), is the developer's identity: "Geva Kipper", accompanied by a pulsing red "REC" dot. Directly beneath his name is a sleek, monospaced line reading: "Currently engineering scalable software solutions at Google."
In the top-right corner, mimicking a camera warning or mode indicator, is the subtitle: "AUTO-GEN MODE ACTIVE: This interface is hallucinated and re-designed daily by AI, resulting in an ever-changing digital mutation."
The bottom corners feature classic camera battery icons and exposure meters, but they dynamically react to the user’s scrolling speed and scroll depth.

**Navigation & Interaction Mechanics**
The user navigates the single-page site via horizontal scrolling, which simulates the camera panning across a dark gallery to "snap" photos. The eight content sections exist as floating, high-resolution "snapshots" arranged in a horizontal filmstrip. As a new snapshot enters the center of the screen, an "autofocus" animation occurs: a digital square brackets `[   ]` locks onto the image with a subtle green flash, and the image transitions from a heavy Gaussian blur to razor-sharp focus, accompanied by a faint mechanical lens-focusing sound effect. 

**The Eight "Snapshots" (Content Sections)**
Each of the eight sections is styled as a digital photograph embedded with EXIF metadata. The project image/visual sits in the center, while the text descriptions and links are overlaid along the edges of the image in a tiny, crisp, tech-style typography.

*   **Snapshot 1 (GlglStats):** The visual is an abstract, colorful representation of a soundwave or LZ77 compression pattern. The text describes the Israeli pop-chart repetition analysis. Two distinct camera-button UI elements are overlaid: one linking to `https://glglstats.geekip.dev` and a "PLAY" button icon linking to the PyData TLV conference video on YouTube.
*   **Snapshot 2 (Ahuzat Dibuk):** The visual is a vibrant, glowing heat-map of Tel-Aviv. The text outlines the parking lot scraping data. Links to the news coverage (13tv, Mako) and the Secret Tel-Aviv Facebook post are styled as "MEDIA SOURCES" in a dropdown-like camera menu.
*   **Snapshot 3 (2D Part Orienting):** The visual is an animated, wireframe polygon rotating into a proper orientation. The text details the C++ computational geometry push-plan algorithm, with a "SOURCE" button linking to the TAU CGL project page.
*   **Snapshot 4 (The Turing Jest):** Presented as a split-screen battle visual. The text describes the LMSYS-like arena for LLM pun battles, with an "ENTER ARENA" button pointing to `https://jest.geekip.dev`.
*   **Snapshot 5 (Rhymes of Israel):** Features a collage of classic highschool yearbook photos overlaid with torn newsprint. The description covers the LLM Chrome extension, with a "DOWNLOAD EXTENSION" button pointing to the Chrome Web Store.
*   **Snapshot 6 (Hatul Basak):** Displays an intricate, interconnected web of Wikipedia puzzle pieces. The text explains the Hebrew catfishing.net port and its AI/user-generated puzzles. A "PLAY DAILY PUZZLE" button links to `https://hatulbasak.geekip.dev`.
*   **Snapshot 7 (Meta Portfolio):** The visual is an "infinity mirror" effect—a camera screen showing a camera screen, representing the site itself. The description points out the meta nature of the project, with a self-referential link to `https://geekip.dev`.
*   **Snapshot 8 (Social Connections):** Designed as the camera's "Export/Share" menu. It contains three large, sleek QR-code-style buttons or minimalist icons for GitHub (github.com/gevak), LinkedIn (linkedin.com/in/gevakip), and Facebook (facebook.com/geva.kipper).

**The Footer Timecode**
Anchoring the bottom center of the viewfinder HUD is a continuous, ticking video timecode. It features the credit: "RENDERED BY GPT-4o" (or the respective AI model name). Right next to it is a live stopwatch displaying the exact hours, minutes, and seconds elapsed since the anchor timestamp: `2026-08-09 01:09:47`. This timecode ticks upward in real-time, grounding the ephemeral AI-generated concept in a precise, relentless digital clock.
