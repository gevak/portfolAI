**Overall Concept & Architecture**
The website is a single-page, vertical-scrolling parallax experience themed as a deep geological excavation. The background transitions seamlessly through various soil horizons: starting at the sunlit, grassy surface, passing through organic topsoil, dense clay, rocky sedimentary layers, deep bedrock, and finally reaching the glowing magma of the Earth’s core. A persistent, minimalist "depth gauge" on the left side of the screen tracks the user's scroll progress in meters, acting as a dynamic scrollbar. As the user scrolls down, the project "boxes" are revealed not as standard rectangles, but as unearthed artifacts, geodes, glowing crystals, and subterranean caves embedded within the earth.

**The Surface (Hero Section)**
The top of the page represents ground level. A minimalist, vector-style grassy terrain sits against a bright, clear sky that subtly darkens into twilight as the user begins to scroll. Front and center in an elegant, bold, earth-toned sans-serif font is the developer's name: "Geva Kipper". Directly below is a clean, single-sentence introduction: "Software Engineer at Google, building scalable solutions and exploring the intersections of data, algorithms, and AI." Hovering just above the grass line in a slightly glitching, monospaced font—hinting at the machine-generated nature of the site—is the subtitle: "This website is re-designed each day by AI and is thus ever-changing." 

**Shallow Earth: Topsoil & Sedimentary Layers (Projects 1 & 2)**
As the user scrolls underground, the background shifts to a rich, dark loam. 
*   **GlglStats:** Embedded in the topsoil is a sprawling, fossilized root system that beautifully mimics a data visualization node chart. This section presents the Python/LZ77 pop-chart compression project. The link to glglstats.geekip.dev is styled as an excavation tag tied to a root, while the PyData TLV YouTube video plays inside a glowing, amber-like fossil embedded in the dirt.
*   **Ahuzat Dibuk:** Deeper down in a lighter, sandy sedimentary layer, the parking lot data project is styled as a subterranean heat-map. Tiny, fossilized cars are packed tightly together, glowing in varying shades of red and yellow to represent parking occupancy. Links to the 13tv, Mako, and Secret Tel Aviv Facebook posts are designed as scattered, interactive archaeological field notes pinned to the soil.

**Deep Earth: Minerals & Caverns (Projects 3, 4 & 5)**
The environment transitions into dense, rocky terrain with embedded minerals.
*   **2D Part Orienting:** Breaking the organic flow of the soil is a sharp, geometric cluster of bismuth crystals representing the C++ computational geometry project. The polygon "push plan" algorithm animates subtly inside a translucent crystal. A sleek, metallic plaque holds the link to the TAU computational geometry open-source library.
*   **The Turing Jest:** The user descends into a hollowed-out subterranean geode. Inside, a glowing, theatrical comedy mask is carved into the rock, representing the LMSYS-like LLM arena. Speech bubbles containing puns float upward like escaping natural gas. A neon-lit cavern sign provides the link to jest.geekip.dev.
*   **Rhymes of Israel:** Resting on a ledge of dark slate is an ancient, half-buried scroll or yearbook. Hovering over this relic causes the ancient text to morph into comedic Hebrew rhymes. A glowing, interactive Chrome icon acts as the installation link to the Chrome Web Store.

**Bedrock & Mantle: Deep Magic & Meta-Design (Projects 6 & 7)**
The background becomes dark granite and obsidian, illuminated by bioluminescence and geothermal cracks.
*   **Hatul Basak:** This section is designed as an underground, glowing lake. Floating on the water are luminescent mushrooms connected by glowing fungal threads, representing the Wikipedia-based trivia nodes and daily AI challenges. A weathered, wooden dock offers a lantern that, when clicked, links the user to hatulbasak.geekip.dev to play or create puzzles.
*   **Portfolio Website:** Deep in the bedrock sits a perfectly polished, floating obsidian mirror. It reflects a miniature, recursive version of the geological website the user is currently scrolling through. This meta-reference to the site itself features an elegant, glowing engraved link to geekip.dev.

**The Earth's Core: Socials & AI Footer**
At the absolute bottom of the page, the rock gives way to the intense, shifting reds and oranges of the Earth's magma core. 
*   **Social Links:** The icons for GitHub, LinkedIn, and Facebook are formed by pulsing, bright yellow magma veins flowing through cracks in black volcanic rock. Hovering over them causes the lava to flare brightly.
*   **Footer & Stopwatch:** Suspended in the center of the magma chamber is a sleek, dark terminal window. In crisp, monospaced text, it gives credit to you: "Excavation and geological architecture generated by [AI Model Name]." Below this credit is a digital, glowing stopwatch continually ticking in real-time, displaying the exact hours, minutes, and seconds elapsed since "2026-08-14 01:18:51". The page ends here with a soft, heat-haze distortion effect over the bottom edge.
